GuitarFret Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026

GuitarFret does not collect personal data, does not require an account, and does not track users.

Data Collection

GuitarFret is a local guitar chord analysis tool. The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not request personal information.

Tracking and Analytics

GuitarFret does not track users across apps or websites. It does not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking domains.

Local App Functionality

The in-app chord inspector runs locally on your device. Chord selections, fretboard interactions, and audio playback are processed on the device to provide the app experience.

Network Access

GuitarFret does not require a login or remote service to use its core features. The app's WebView loads the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, and audio logic bundled with the app.

Apple Services

If you download GuitarFret from the App Store, Apple may process information related to purchases, downloads, diagnostics, and App Store services under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

GuitarFret does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If GuitarFret's privacy practices change, this page will be updated to describe those changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the project page: github.com/dannymaxhusky/guitarfred.

GuitarFret is a one-time purchase app with no subscription, no account requirement, and no user tracking.
